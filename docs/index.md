# OpenLabel documentation

OpenLabel: product labels for Magento 2 and Hyvä. Free, MIT, Hyvä-native, one query per page, zero layout shift. Labels on listings, product pages, the cart, mini cart, wish list, compare list and order emails.

- [Install and upgrade](install.md): requirements, the three commands, production mode, upgrades, uninstall.
- [Admin](admin.md): starters, the labels grid, the label form with live preview, designs, settings and permissions.
- [Storefront](storefront.md): markup and CSS contract, the generated stylesheet, caching and customer groups, right-to-left stores, custom themes.
- [CLI](cli.md): every `openlabel:` command.
- [GraphQL](graphql.md): the `openlabel_labels` field on products, visibility rules and caching.
- [REST API](rest-api.md): create, read, change and delete labels and designs from a PIM, ERP or script.
- [Export and import](import-export.md): moving labels between environments, and from the Smile product label module.
- [The engine](engine.md): data model, conditions, indexer, variables and resolver, for developers.
- [Hyvä integration](hyva-integration.md): how labels are injected into Hyvä listings and the product gallery.
